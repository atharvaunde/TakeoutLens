AU
Atharva Unde
atharva@thecareerlabs.com
Apr 20, 2023

Hello Team,

I wanted to share with you the results of our recent load tests on the HikariCP issues we encountered with the rule engine.

We conducted tests on two different environments

1. docker-swarm based architecture that ran 10 replicas of the rule engine.
2. docker-compose based architecture that ran only 1 replica.

After analyzing the results, we found that the swarm-based architecture is more sustainable to varying demands. We plan to load test the swarm architecture further this month and aim to push the changes to production by early May.
